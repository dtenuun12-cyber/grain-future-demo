Our story

We got tired of furniture that doesn't last a decade.

GRAIN started in a small workshop outside Johor Bahru, built on one complaint: most furniture today is engineered to be replaced, not repaired.

Particleboard swells. Veneer chips. Glued joints fail the moment you move house. We wanted to build the opposite — furniture that a future owner could sand down and refinish in twenty years and have it look better than the day it left our workshop.

Every piece we make is solid timber, air-dried on our own schedule rather than kiln-forced, and joined using methods that don't rely on glue alone to hold weight. It costs more to build this way. It also means we've never had a piece come back broken.

We're a small team — which is also why we built the assistant you'll see on this site. It knows our full catalog, our materials, and our process, so you can get a straight answer at 11pm without waiting for us to open.

How we build

From tree to table

01

Selecting the timber

We choose boards for grain and stability, not just for how they look on day one.

02

Air drying

Slow drying over months, not kiln-forced in days — it's why our pieces don't warp.

03

Joinery

Mortise and tenon, dovetails where it counts. Screws only where they're actually right.

04

Finishing

Hand-oiled, not lacquered — so the wood can be refreshed for decades, not replaced.
