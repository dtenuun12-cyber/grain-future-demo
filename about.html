We build furniture from whole timber in Johor Bahru, designed to last generations rather than seasons.

GRAIN was founded on a simple frustration: the ubiquity of flat-pack particleboard and synthetic veneers that end up in landfills within a few years. We believe furniture should have a lineage.

Every piece is crafted using air-dried domestic hardwoods—Oak, Teak, Walnut, and Ash—joined with traditional mortise-and-tenon construction that accommodates the natural movement of timber.

Our workshop is open to visitors by appointment. Come feel the weight of the wood, inspect the hand-rubbed oil finishes, and discuss custom dimensions for your home.
