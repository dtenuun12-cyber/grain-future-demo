Process

From tree to table

01

Selecting the timber

We choose boards for grain and stability, not just for how they look on day one.

02

Air drying

Slow drying over months, not kiln-forced in days — it's why our pieces don't warp.

03

Joinery

Mortise and tenon, dovetails where it counts. Screws only where they're actually right.

04

Finishing

Hand-oiled, not lacquered — so the wood can be refreshed for decades, not replaced.
